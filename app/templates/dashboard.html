Metric conventions

Price chart
Drawn from one adjustment basis, named on the card — Yahoo's auto-adjusted closes (full history, so its high really is an all-time high), or the ≤1000-bar Longbridge forward-adjusted window until that fetch lands. The two are never stitched: the step on the changeover date would be an artefact, not a move. Running high is an expanding max and the SMA a full-series mean, both computed over the whole series and then windowed to the last {{ params.chart_window_sessions }} sessions shown, so neither resets at the left edge. The right edge is the last daily close; the live price in the panel heading is a different series and is not patched onto the line. Where both bases are available their SMA{{ params.sma_window }}s are compared, and a gap wider than {{ settings.sma_cross_source_tolerance_pct }}% is flagged rather than averaged away.
Drawdown
Close-based, against the expanding max of the fetched window (max 1000 daily bars ≈ 4 years — not a true all-time high). The 52-week figure is reported separately.
SMA{{ params.sma_window }}
Simple mean of the last {{ params.sma_window }} trading-day closes. Below {{ params.sma_window }} valid closes it is left blank with the actual n, never shown as a partial mean.
RV{{ params.rv_window }}
Zero-mean estimator sqrt(mean(r²))·sqrt(252) over {{ params.rv_window }} log returns — the variance-swap-consistent form, which is the correct thing to compare against implied vol.
DTE
Calendar days in America/New_York, expiry day = 0, T = DTE/365. Expiries are screened in the {{ params.dte_min }}–{{ params.dte_max }} DTE window.
Greeks
Black-76, computed locally from the API's implied vol. The API's own theta is unusable — its sign flips across moneyness — so it is not used. The API's delta is kept only as a health check (flagged above a 0.02 difference).
Forward
Implied by put-call parity from ATM mid prices, which avoids assuming a dividend yield. Risk-free rate: {{ rate.label }}.
Theta / Vega
Theta is shown positive for a seller (decay is income). Vega is per share per 1 IV point; the per-contract column is ×100.
Premium
At mid, as a percent of strike; annualized simple (×365/DTE), not compounded. The bid-based figure is the conservative case.
IV vs RV
Uses ATM IV, never the sold put's own IV, because skew biases the wing high by construction and would fake a variance risk premium.
IV Rank
Where 30-day ATM IV sits in its trailing 252-session range, computed within a single source — mixing raw IV levels across sources would inject a fake vol jump on the changeover date. Preference: our own daily recording once it spans a year, else the matching vol index (VIX/VXN/RVX/VXD), else DoltHub's published 52-week range. A vol index is a whole-strip variance measure running ≈1.19× this ticker's ATM IV, so only its rank transfers; its level is never shown as the ticker's IV. Greyed out below {{ settings.min_iv_history_days }} observations.
Liquidity
Liquid when absolute spread ≤ {{ '%g'|format(params.max_abs_spread) }} or relative ≤ {{ '%g'|format(params.max_rel_spread_pct) }}% of mid. A zero bid is never liquid, since there is no exit.
Data source
Longbridge (LongPort) OpenAPI. Prices stream by push; greeks are polled every {{ settings.greeks_poll_seconds|int }}s because option quote requests are capped at 500 symbols per rolling minute.
